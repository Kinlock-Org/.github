# M0-03 — Attester conversations

**Goal:** talk to at least 2 attesters (associations, NGOs) about accountability and process.
**Feeds:** `PRD.md` §8.2 trigger ("fewer than 2 attesters willing to put their name on payee verification" → stop, the trust model has no supply side") and §10 assumption 2.
**Done when:** named willing attesters, or a documented "no."

## Who to recruit

- A school association, landlord/property association, local NGO, or similar body with standing to vouch for institutions in a candidate market — ideally one per candidate market, at least 2 total.
- Needs to be an organization (or named individual with organizational backing) willing to be publicly listed as the attester for payees it verifies — not an anonymous reference.

## Script

1. **Role fit.** What's your organization's current process for vetting or vouching for schools/landlords (if any)? Who do you already verify, and how?
2. **The ask, explained plainly.** Attesters on Kinlock publicly vouch for a payee (school/landlord): verify identity and legitimacy, set the payout address, and can update status (active/suspended/revoked) if something goes wrong. Your name is shown on every payee you verify. Does that match something you'd be comfortable doing?
3. **Accountability.** If a payee you verified turned out to be fraudulent or mismanaged funds, what should happen? What recourse or process would you want in place (e.g., ability to revoke, public record of the revocation)?
4. **Process and effort.** Walk through what verifying a new payee would realistically take on your end — documentation, in-person visits, time per payee. Is there a volume you could sustainably handle?
5. **Risk appetite.** Is there anything about having your name permanently, publicly attached to a verification that concerns you (reputational, legal, safety)?
6. **Commitment.** Would your organization be willing to be a named attester for the testnet pilot? If not now, what would need to be true first?
