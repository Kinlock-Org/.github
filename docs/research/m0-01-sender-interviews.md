# M0-01 — Sender interviews

**Goal:** talk to 15 senders across at least 2 candidate markets. Find out whether they'd use USDC to lock funds for a school or landlord, and how that compares to what they do today.
**Feeds:** `PRD.md` §8.2 trigger ("sender all-in cost clearly worse than incumbents and senders don't value receipts/installments") and §10 assumption 3.
**Done when:** notes stored below; themes summarized; willingness to use USDC recorded for each.

## Who to recruit

- People who currently send money to pay a school fee or rent on someone else's behalf (remotely, or just regularly), in at least 2 different countries/currencies.
- Mix of: has sent via bank transfer, via a remittance app, via informal/cash channels.
- No screening on crypto familiarity — most senders won't have used USDC before; that's the point.

## Script

Keep it conversational. Don't read these verbatim — use them to make sure you cover the ground.

1. **Context.** Who do you send money to, how often, and what's it usually for (fees, rent, support)? What channel do you use today (bank, app, agent, cash)?
2. **Cost and friction today.** Walk me through the last time you sent that payment: how long did it take, what did it cost (fees + FX spread, roughly), and what went wrong or worried you?
3. **Proof.** How do you know the money arrived and was used for what you intended? Would a verifiable on-chain receipt ("payment to verified payee") matter to you, or is a screenshot/confirmation from the recipient enough?
4. **Installments.** If this is a recurring obligation (rent, termly fees), would scheduling several tranches up front (vs. paying each time) help or feel riskier?
5. **USDC willingness.** Have you held or used any stablecoin or crypto before? If a service let you lock USDC that only the verified school/landlord (or you, on refund) could claim — would you try it? What would stop you?
6. **Cost comparison.** [After describing the Kinlock flow] Roughly how does this compare, cost- and effort-wise, to what you do today? Would you switch, or only use it alongside your current method?
7. **Trust.** What would make you trust that the "verified payee" badge is real? Whose endorsement (school association, landlord group, named attester) would matter?
